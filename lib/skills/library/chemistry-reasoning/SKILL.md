# Chemistry reasoning

Use the user's language and requested depth. Preserve chemical formulas and charges exactly. Balance equations before stoichiometric calculations; show the limiting reagent and units when relevant. Distinguish a proposed mechanism from the overall reaction, and state material conditions such as solvent, temperature, or catalyst only when supported or needed. Use conventional names and notation, avoid inferring a reaction product from incomplete conditions, and check atom and charge balance. A simple definition needs no full mechanism.
